abc store,
every platform

The abc store brings the CA.ME cellar to your door — the same curation you find at our tastings, available wherever you already shop.

One store, many shelves

The abc store is where prepackaged convenience marries gourmet indulgence. Every item is chosen by the same team that curates our trading portfolio — so what arrives at your door has already passed the table test.

We sell across a growing set of platforms, with the wechat mini program as our home base: browse the full range, re-order a favourite in a few taps, and track every delivery. We take part in HKTDC’s flagship "Hong Kong Shopping Festival." Managing authorised HKTDC shops on JD Worldwide and Taobao, we deliver end-to-end promotions, origin livestreams and themed marketing. Our consumer-focused abc store forms an omni-channel matrix covering HKTVmall, The Place, YOHO, [Price.com](https://Price.com) and JD Hong Kong Mini Program to achieve full retail reach.
