How can Camenae Asia help an international brand enter China?

Camenae Asia makes Hong Kong your first stop in China — company incorporation, brand credentials, market research, positioning, channels and media, all under one roof. Digital branding sits at the core, from multilingual websites to AI search placement, so your brand shows up in both search results and AI answers.

Can you represent our wine or food brand and handle distribution in China?

Yes — agency and distribution are the core of our global trade business. We select the world's finest producers with a rigorous eye and represent a portfolio of European wine estates, building a direct path from origin to shelf across fine wines, quality food and wellness living.

What major events and brand projects has Camenae Asia delivered?

Camenae Asia delivers full-scale events from strategy to execution. We have run the Asia Sommelier Summit and China's Sommelier Championship, and created HK Day Chongqing — a 5,000 m² immersive space bringing together 30+ Hong Kong brands and drawing over 7,000 visitors in a single day.

What is the abc store, and how is it different from a regular import shop?

The abc store is Camenae Asia's own retail brand — and a working example of how we take a brand from selection and positioning into different market channels. Through online platforms and a WeChat mini program, it brings prepackaged convenience and gourmet indulgence straight to consumers, from fine wines to quality food and wellness products.

How do we start working with Camenae Asia?

Send us a note through the contact page with a little about your brand, target market and goals. Camenae Asia will reply and usually arrange a short meeting, online or in person, then propose a market entry and growth plan once we understand your goals.
