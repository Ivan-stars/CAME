Our Story

Since our inception in 2009, Camenae Asia has been on a mission to elevate the art of living. Our commitment to professionalism, innovation, and fostering long-term relationships is the cornerstone of our operations.

We excel in trading exceptional food and wine, bringing the globe’s finest to your table. Our brand consultancy services go beyond standard advice, creating immersive experiences that blend professional wisdom with leisurely pleasure — extended today through our academy, our events practice, and the abc store. Mission Connecting premium global resources, we empower brands to achieve long-term growth through end-to-end brand services and a curated selection of high-quality lifestyle products, while elevating the everyday living experience for consumers. Vision Guided by the core philosophy of "The Art of Quality Living," we have established a deep presence in the Greater Bay Area while extending our reach across the Asia-Pacific market. We serve as the preferred partner for international brands entering China, distinguished by both our professional depth and the breadth of our business ecosystem.

End-to-End Closed-Loop Expertise

We break boundaries of trade, marketing, channels and scenarios. Our one-stop service spans global sourcing, import, branding, channel launch and experience operation, distinct from standalone traders or marketing firms.

Professional Endorsement

Our core team holds official certifications and experience running top-tier competitions. We carry solid influence in wine and hospitality, earning lasting trust from brands.

Cross-Border Hub Integration

Rooted in Hong Kong’s international trade hub, we understand cross-border business rules and consumer cultures. We reliably localise overseas brands entering the Chinese market.

Established Premium Resource

With 20+ years in the industry, we have accumulated premium brands, high-end channels and elite networks, ready to mobilise resources for large-scale projects.
