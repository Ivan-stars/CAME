Premier Industry Competitions

We have consecutively delivered flagship events including the Asian Top Sommelier Summit and China Sommelier Competition. These gatherings bring together sommelier presidents, industry associations and suppliers from 12 Asian countries, hosting over 200 guests. Covered by CCTV, the events serve as a professional exchange hub for Asia’s wine community.

Cultural-Tourism Flagship Projects

Immersive Gastronomy Experience in Chongqing. Set within a 5,000 sqm venue, the project featured over 30 Hong Kong brands, with creative programmes including cosplay showcases and tasting pairings. It attracted more than 7,000 visitors in a single day, acting as a cross-border cultural exchange showcase.

City-Scale Flagship Events

We are deeply involved in signature city events such as Hong Kong Wine & Dine Festival. We are well-versed in Hong Kong’s compliance requirements, venue management and merchant collaboration frameworks for large-scale festivals.

Themed Experience Workshops

We curate intimate tasting salons including “Simply HH with Wine”. These interactive sessions connect prospective clients in a relaxed setting, strengthen brand affinity and nurture loyal communities.
